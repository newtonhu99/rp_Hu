Materials and Procedure

The materials used and procedure followed for this project

Materials

  • Scalpel
  • Twenty 100mm petri dishes, Carolina Biological
  • Planaria, G. tigrina, Carolina Biological
  • Beef liver to feed the planaria
  • Spring water
  • Erythritol
  • Microscope
  • Metric Scales

Step

Description

Image

1

Gather Materials for Experiment

  • Obtained a tank of planaria from Dr. Ellsworth
  • Obtained erythritol from Walmart
  • Obtained spring water from the lab
  • Obtained petri dishes and all external tools from the lab

2

Set up the Experimental Environment

  • Created 5 different aqueous solutions of different concentrations of erythritol
  • There was 1 control group and 4 experimental groups
  • Put them all into 10 petri dishes, 5 for heads, 5 for tails
  • Created ice plates for cutting

3

Begin the Data Collection Process

  • Cut 15 planaria worms into 15 heads and tails
  • Placed 3 heads or 3 tails in each petri dish
  • Measured the initial lengths of the heads and tails

4

Data Collection

  • Took daily measurements of all of the planaria.
  • To measure the planaria, a picture was taken with a ruler beneath the petri dish
  • Then, photoshop software was used to determine the lengths
  • Daily measurements continued for 15 days
  • Lengths were recorded in lab notebook

5

Data Analysis

  • Copied all raw data from lab notebook to spreadsheet
  • Calculated the average growth length across all of the groups
  • Performed a T-test on the average growth values
  • P-values were analyzed, p>0.05 = not significant
